SupportOps Agent FAQ

退款处理：
用户申请退款时，先核验订单号、支付状态、发货状态和退款资格。
如果问题涉及投诉、强烈不满、重复扣款或支付失败，应标记为中高风险，
必要时转人工客服继续核实。

支付失败但已扣款：
如果用户反馈付款失败、银行卡或支付渠道已经扣款，请先安抚用户，
再收集订单号、支付渠道、支付时间和扣款截图。客服需要核实订单是否创建成功，
如确认重复扣款或异常扣款，应协助用户发起退款或转人工处理。

账号安全与隐私：
涉及账号被盗、异常登录、密码重置、隐私信息、身份证号、手机号或银行卡号时，
不要要求用户在公开渠道发送完整敏感信息，应转人工安全专员处理。

物流延迟：
如果订单未发货或物流长时间未更新，客服应查询物流节点，说明预计处理方式，
并根据平台规则判断是否需要补偿、改地址或转人工。

Refund policy:
If a customer asks for a refund, first verify the order number, payment status,
and refund eligibility. Refund-related or complaint-related cases should be
marked as medium or high risk and transferred to a human agent when necessary.

Payment failure:
If payment failed but money was deducted, ask the customer to provide the order
number, payment channel, payment time, and screenshot. Check whether the order
was created successfully before suggesting repayment.

Account security:
For suspicious login, password reset, account takeover, privacy, or identity
verification issues, protect user data and transfer the case to a human agent.

Shipping delay:
If an order has not shipped or delivery is delayed, check the logistics status
and tell the customer whether to wait, update the address, or contact support.
